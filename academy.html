01 Why us为什么是我们

Software vendor, project archive, employer — in one place 软件商、案例库、用人方，在同一家

A software vendor sells you licenses. A consultancy writes the syllabus. A staffing agency places the graduates. Those are usually three different companies, which is why most campus partnerships stall right after the software is installed. 软件商提供授权，咨询机构提供课程大纲，人力机构对接就业。三者通常由不同主体分别承担，校企合作因此多停留在软件部署阶段。

02 The teaching model岗课赛证

Post, course, contest, certificate 以岗定标，四位一体

The job description sets the standard; the course carries it; the competition proves it; the certificate records it. Run in that order, a cohort comes out matched to a real hiring profile instead of a software manual. 岗位定标准，课程承载它，竞赛检验它，证书记录它。按这个顺序走，学生对上的是真实用人画像，而不是一本软件手册。

By post — set the standard以岗定标
We survey the actual digital-transformation roles being hired for and turn the hiring criteria straight into the training plan. 深度调研数字化转型一线岗位的真实人才模型，把行业用人标准与岗位考核要求直接引入培养方案。
By course — teach it以课育才
Full software licenses, de-identified real project drawings and courseware, with our engineers co-teaching. The classroom runs on live material. 配发全套自主软件、脱密的真实企业项目图纸与课件，工程师参与课程共建，实现课堂即实战。
By contest — prove it以赛促学
Official technical support partner for the BRICS Future Skills “Good Housing” digital construction design contest; coaching from team selection through the finals. 金砖国家职业技能大赛"好房子"数字建造设计大赛官方技术合作支撑单位，从校内选拔到冲刺全流程带赛。
By certificate — record it以证提质
Skill-level certification in digital construction, administered with the national certification body and industry leaders, so the credential carries weight with an employer. 联合职业技能培训认证中心与头部企业开展数字建造职业技能等级认证，让证书在用人单位那里有分量。

03 Cooperation5+N 合作体系

Five programs. Start with any one of them 五大方案，从任意一个开始

A department is rarely rebuilt in one step. Start with the program that fits where you are, prove it works, then widen the scope. 专业建设通常不宜一次性重构。建议选择与现有基础匹配的切入点，验证成效后再逐步扩展合作范围。

01

Program development专业建设

Micro-majors around “good housing” and urban renewal, dropped into an existing curriculum; employer-sponsored cohorts; full industry-college partnerships. 围绕"好房子"与城市更新方向共建微专业，轻量嵌入现有培养方案；企业订单班联合招生；共建产业学院。

02

Courses & materials精品课程

Online courses built to provincial and national award standards, DFC courseware, lab task sheets, test banks, loose-leaf and digital textbooks, and the ten-module project case pack. 对标省级国家级的精品在线课程、DFC 数字化课程资源、实训任务单与题库、活页与数字教材，以及十大模块项目案例包。

03

Faculty development师资提升

A one- to two-month technical residency per instructor each year on our live projects, plus industry mentors embedded in the department. 每年安排专业教师赴企业开展 1–2 个月技术研修，参与技术攻关；设立产业导师特聘岗驻校任教。

04

Competitions学科竞赛

A full prep package for the BRICS contest, plus WorldSkills-track, national, and provincial events, and regional contests co-hosted on campus. 金砖大赛全套备赛资源，覆盖世职赛、国赛、行业赛与省赛；校企联合在校内承办区域赛事。

05

Labs & joint centers产学合作

A jointly funded training lab, a campus studio running small live commissions, and a joint DFC research center that takes on outside work. 共建标准化综合实训基地；校内工作坊承接中小型真实项目；共建挂牌 DFC 产学研中心，可承接外部业务。

Research grant applications, dual-credential tracks, occupational standards development, and school-break intensives that end with a portfolio. 创新课题申报、双证融通认证、行业标准编写，以及产出作品集的假期岗位能力集训。
