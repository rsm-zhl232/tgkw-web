01 Capabilities服务能力

01

Nested design嵌套设计

An existing drawing set is rebuilt as one coordinated model across every discipline. Errors, omissions and clashes are identified systematically, each assigned an owner and a resolution date, producing a design baseline all parties confirm before any trade mobilises. 在既有施工图基础上重建为全专业协同模型，系统识别错、漏、碰、缺，每一项指定责任人与处理时限。在各专业进场之前，形成一份各方共同确认的设计基准。

  • Full-discipline nested and detailed design全专业嵌套设计与深化设计
  • Clash review, multi-party resolution, issue close-out碰撞检查、多方评审决策、问题闭环归零
  • Interfaces and responsibilities defined in the model and the schedule界面与责任在模型和清单中提前界定

02

Forward design正向设计

From scheme or design development onward, the DFC model is the single source of record; DWG drawings, the bill of quantities, the material schedule and the cutting list are all exports from it. A change to the model updates all four, moving cost control forward into design rather than leaving it as a downstream audit. 自方案或扩初阶段起，DFC 模型即为唯一数据源，DWG 施工图、工程量清单、物料表与下料单均由其导出。模型一经修改，四类成果同步更新，成本控制因此前置为设计阶段的决策依据，而非事后核对环节。

  • Forward documentation for architecture, MEP, fit-out and façade建筑、机电、装饰、幕墙施工图正向设计
  • Quantities and material schedules from the same model工程量清单与物料表同源导出
  • Design-management scope for the whole package设计总包服务

03

On-site detailing施工现场深化

As-built structures always deviate from drawn dimensions. We survey the structure by point cloud, reconcile the model to within 5 mm, then set out with a layout robot. Six-face alignment in the fit-out is achieved through this step rather than by trimming components on site. 实际建成结构与图纸尺寸始终存在偏差。我们对已建结构进行点云扫描，将模型与点云校准至 5mm 以内，再由放线机器人完成现场定位。室内六面对缝的精度由此环节保障，而非依靠现场裁切修正。

  • Point-cloud survey and reverse modelling点云扫描与逆向建模
  • Robotic setting-out, panel layout and factory cutting files机器人放线、排版下单与工厂下料
  • Resident engineers through the construction phase施工阶段驻场服务

04

Construction management施工管理服务

Drawing review, programme and quality control, variation and cost management are all run against the single model rather than a set of separate documents. Available on a cost-plus-fee basis, aligning our return with the project outcome. 图纸审核、进度与质量管控、变更与成本管理均基于统一模型执行，而非分散的图纸文件。可采用成本加酬金模式，使服务方收益与项目效益直接挂钩。

  • Drawing audit, supplier submissions, interface pre-emption全专业图纸审核、供应商图纸整合、冲突预判
  • Measured survey, three-tier programme control, hidden-works inspection实测实量复核、三级进度管控、隐蔽工程监督
  • Digital cost management across the whole process工程造价数字化管理
