Ten years, one idea 十年，一件事

The company was registered in Shenzhen in September 2015 as Qiaoduo Tiangong, working in architectural fit-out. Commercial tools could not sustain the cost pressure and delivery pace of that market, so the team began developing its own. In December 2021 the shareholding was restructured and Tiangong Kaiwu was formed to productise them. 公司前身"巧夺天宫"于 2015 年 9 月在深圳注册，主营建筑装饰业务。面对国内装饰行业高成本压力与高交付节奏，既有商业软件难以支撑实际生产需求，团队因此启动自主工具研发。2021 年 12 月完成股权重置，设立天宫开物，将内部工具正式产品化。

Over ¥100m has since gone into DFC, TAI and ADC, along one consistent technical line: a single data model carried from the first sketch through to handover, rather than a set of independent point tools. 此后在 DFC、TAI 与 ADC 上的累计投入超过 1 亿元，技术路线始终一致：以一套贯穿从方案草图到竣工交付全过程的数据模型为核心，而非提供彼此独立的单点工具。
