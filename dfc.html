Traditional BIM asks you to redraw everything, three times 传统 BIM 流程，让你把同一件事画三遍

Model in one tool, clash-check in another, document in a third, then rebuild the quantities in a fourth. Each hop loses information and adds a week. DFC collapses that chain into a single parallel workflow — which is why a fit-out team can get from concept to issued drawings without handing the model over at all. 在一个软件建模、另一个软件查碰撞、第三个软件出图、第四个软件算量。每一次转换都在丢信息、加工期。DFC 把这条链路压缩为一次并行流程——装饰团队从方案到出图，全程不必移交模型。

03 The DFC workflowDFC 新流程

  1. Model once一次建模

    Architecture, structure, MEP, fit-out, façade and landscape build into the same lightweight file — parametric objects that carry material, spec and supplier, not just geometry. 建筑、结构、机电、装饰、幕墙、园林建在同一个轻量化文件里。构件带材料、规格与供应商信息，而不只是几何体。

  2. Coordinate in the cloud云端协同

    Clash detection and review happen against the live shared model, not an exported snapshot. 碰撞检测与审图基于实时共享模型，而非导出的快照。

  3. Document forward正向出图

    Plans, sections and details are laid out and annotated from the model and issued as DWG. 平立剖与详图由模型排版标注，导出 DWG 施工图。

  4. Quantify and procure算量与招采

    Bills of quantity and material schedules export with the drawings, tied to real products in the library. 工程量清单与物料表随图导出，并关联天宫库中的真实产品。

  5. Build to the model按模型施工

    Point-cloud survey and robotic layout tie the model back to the site for accurate setting-out and cutting. 配合点云扫描与机器人放线，精准定位、精准下料。

04 One model, five states一模到底的五个版本

The model never restarts. It gets more certain 模型不重来，只是越来越确定

A project does not run model → drawings → site. It runs through one model that hardens in five steps, each closing with a review the client signs off. Nothing is handed over, so nothing is lost in the handover. 项目不是"建模 → 出图 → 施工"，而是同一个模型分五步逐级变硬，每一步以一次业主签字的评审收尾。没有移交，也就没有移交时丢掉的东西。

  1. 0.5   Expose暴露问题

    The existing design is rebuilt as a model and clash-checked. Errors, omissions and conflicts are reported; the modelling baseline is agreed. 把原设计重建为模型并做碰撞检查，系统汇报错漏碰缺，确立建模基准与修改原则。

  2. 1.0   Freeze协调归零

    Three rounds of full-discipline coordination take conflicts to zero. The model is confirmed against the brief and frozen as the tender baseline. 全专业深化与碰撞检测三轮迭代，冲突归零；确认模型与需求一致后冻结，作为招标基准。

  3. 2.0   Detail深化细化

    Junctions, details and trade interfaces are resolved, buildability verified, and the package issued for construction with a technical briefing. 节点、大样与专业界面深化到位，完成可施工性验证，成果打包交付并进行施工交底。

  4. 3.0   Fit to site现场适配

    Point-cloud survey brings the as-built into the model within 5 mm. Setting-out, supplier data and cutting files all follow from the reconciled version. 点云扫描把现场实况纳入模型，偏差控制在 5mm 以内；放线、厂家提资与下料文件均由校准后的版本产生。

  5. 4.0   Hand over数字交付

    The as-built is scanned, reconciled, signed off by all parties and packaged with its data — an asset the owner can operate the building from. 竣工扫描比对、深度完善并多方会审签字，连同数据封装交付——业主拿到的是可用于运营的数字资产。
