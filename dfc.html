DFC. Digital construction, in one model DFC 数字建造平台，一个模型到底

A digital construction platform for architectural design and engineering delivery. 一套面向建筑设计与工程交付的数字建造平台。

DFC connects architecture, structure, MEP, fit-out, and construction detailing through a single model, reducing duplicated modeling and fragmented information, and improving both design coordination and delivery efficiency. DFC 通过统一模型连接建筑、结构、机电、装饰及施工深化等专业环节，减少重复建模和信息割裂，提升设计协同与项目交付效率。

Less duplicated modeling, better cross-discipline coordination 减少重复建模，提升跨专业协同效率

In a conventional workflow, modeling, clash review, documentation, and quantity takeoff each happen in a different tool. Every handover loses information and adds time to the schedule. DFC consolidates that chain into one parallel workflow, so a fit-out team can move from concept to issued drawings without transferring the model between systems. 在传统流程中，建模、碰撞检查、出图与算量分别在不同软件中完成，每一次转换都会造成信息损失并延长工期。DFC 将该链路整合为单一并行流程，装饰专业团队可在不移交模型的前提下，由方案直接推进至施工图交付。

02 Modules功能模块

Five modules, one file 五大模块，同一文件

M01

Architecture & structure建筑与结构

Walls, slabs, openings, and primary and secondary structure. Parametric objects that carry their own data, not just geometry. 墙、板、洞口、一次与二次结构，参数化工具自带信息属性，而不仅是几何体。

M02

Fit-out装饰

Finish faces generated from room boundaries, along with door and window sets, thresholds, sills, and casings. Generated, not drawn. 依据区域与结构信息自动生成天地墙完成面，门窗套、过门石、窗台板一键生成，而非手工绘制。

M03

MEP机电

Water, HVAC, electrical, and fire protection, with routing, clash resolution, and MEP-specific drawing output. 给排水、暖通、电气、消防的路由设计、碰撞处理与机电专项出图。

M04

Documentation & cost出图与造价

Auto-layout of plans, elevations, and sections to DWG; bills of quantities, material schedules, and cutting lists straight from the model. 平、立、剖自动排图导出 DWG；工程量清单、物料统计表、下料排版工单直接由模型生成。

M05

Cloud collaboration云端协同

Split and merge models by discipline and floor, sync progress, and review with markups. Up to 100 people on a single project. 按专业与楼层分模合模，进度同步，审图批注，单项目最多支持 100 人协同。

M06

Tiangong Library天宫库

A materials library of real manufacturer-supplied products, with attributes and supplier data, so a spec resolves to something you can actually buy. 厂商真实产品数字化入库，带专业属性与供应商信息，让选材与清单有据可依。

03 The DFC workflowDFC 新流程

  1. Model once一次建模

    Architecture, structure, MEP, fit-out, façade, and landscape build into the same lightweight file — parametric objects that carry material, spec, and supplier, not just geometry. 建筑、结构、机电、装饰、幕墙、园林建在同一个轻量化文件里。构件带材料、规格与供应商信息，而不只是几何体。

  2. Coordinate in the cloud云端协同

    Clash detection and review happen against the live shared model, not an exported snapshot. 碰撞检测与审图基于实时共享模型，而非导出的快照。

  3. Document forward正向出图

    Plans, sections, and details are laid out and annotated from the model and issued as DWG. 平立剖与详图由模型排版标注，导出 DWG 施工图。

  4. Quantify and procure算量与招采

    Bills of quantities and material schedules export with the drawings, tied to real products in the library. 工程量清单与物料表随图导出，并关联天宫库中的真实产品。

  5. Build to the model按模型施工

    Point-cloud survey and robotic layout tie the model back to the site for accurate layout and cutting. 配合点云扫描与机器人放线，精准定位、精准下料。

04 One model, five states一模到底的五个版本

The model never restarts. It only gets more certain 模型不重来，只是越来越确定

A project does not run model → drawings → site as separate stages. One model advances through five states, each closing with a review the client signs off. Because nothing is handed between systems, no information is lost in transfer. 项目并非"建模 → 出图 → 施工"的分段推进，而是同一模型经历五个确定性递增的版本，每一版以业主签字的评审收尾。全过程不发生系统间移交，因而不产生移交环节的信息损失。

  1. 0.5   Expose暴露问题

    The existing design is rebuilt as a model and clash-checked. Errors, omissions, and conflicts are reported; the modeling baseline is agreed. 把原设计重建为模型并做碰撞检查，系统汇报错漏碰缺，确立建模基准与修改原则。

  2. 1.0   Freeze协调归零

    Three rounds of full-discipline coordination take conflicts to zero. The model is confirmed against the brief and frozen as the tender baseline. 全专业深化与碰撞检测三轮迭代，冲突归零；确认模型与需求一致后冻结，作为招标基准。

  3. 2.0   Detail深化细化

    Junctions, details, and trade interfaces are resolved, buildability verified, and the package issued for construction with a technical briefing. 节点、大样与专业界面深化到位，完成可施工性验证，成果打包交付并进行施工交底。

  4. 3.0   Fit to site现场适配

    Point-cloud survey brings the as-built into the model within 5 mm. Layout, supplier data, and cutting files all follow from the reconciled version. 点云扫描把现场实况纳入模型，偏差控制在 5mm 以内；放线、厂家提资与下料文件均由校准后的版本产生。

  5. 4.0   Hand over数字交付

    The as-built is scanned, reconciled, signed off by all parties, and packaged with its data — an asset the owner can operate the building from. 竣工扫描比对、深度完善并多方会审签字，连同数据封装交付——业主拿到的是可用于运营的数字资产。
